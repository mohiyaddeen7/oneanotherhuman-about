Why this exists

Most social products are built for attention.

OneAnotherHuman is being built for something different: meaningful relationships. A place where you can be yourself, find people who truly get you, and build real connections without turning your life into content or competing for reach.

The long-term idea

A calmer, kinder internet is possible.

We believe meaningful relationships should be a better success metric than another minute of attention. OneAnotherHuman is an attempt to build toward that idea deliberately.
